PROJECT JARVIS: TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURAL DEEP DIVE

Project JARVIS is a state-of-the-art, high-performance AI personal assistant featuring a 3D avatar, advanced computer vision, and a "Trinity Mind" multi-brain architecture.

2. THE TECH STACK
We use a blend of cutting-edge Python for AI/Logical tasks and JavaScript for low-latency 3D rendering.

BACKEND (PYTHON 3.10+)
- Brain: LangGraph + LangChain + Google Gemini Pro (Google-GenerativeAI).
- Computer Vision: DeepFace (Identification), MediaPipe (Landmarking & Tracking), OpenCV (Camera capture).
- Communication: websockets (Real-time tracking), python-telegram-bot (Remote Auth).
- Speech: edge-tts (Premium voices), SpeechRecognition (Google Backend).
- OS Control: pyautogui, psutil, win32com.

FRONTEND (HTML/JS)
- Engine: Three.js (WebGL).
- Avatar Format: VRM (Standard for 3D AI characters).
- Library: @pixiv/three-vrm (Handles bone mapping and expressions).

3. HOW JARVIS "THINKS" (THE TRINITY MIND)
JARVIS doesn't just process text; he routes tasks through three specialized "Minds" using a graph-based orchestrator:

1. Executive Mind (Local): Fast, offline-first logic for simple greetings and direct commands.
2. Analytical Mind (Cloud): Powered by Gemini Pro for complex research, coding, and reasoning.
3. Action Engine: Physical "hands" that can control your PC (Volume, YouTube, WhatsApp).

THE ROUTING LOGIC:
- Router Node: Scans user input for keywords like "research", "remember", "play", or "open".
- Semantic Mapping: Input is classified as Action, Cloud, or Local.
- Emotion Injection: Based on the response, the mind injects an Emotion (Joy, Sorrow, Angry) and a Pose (Thinking, Greeting, Sitting) before sending it to the "Eyes".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE "EYES": BIOMETRIC GUARD & VISION TRACKER
JARVIS sees reality through two distinct pipelines:

- THE IDENTIFIER (BIOMETRIC GUARD): Uses DeepFace (VGG-Face model) to verify if the person at the camera is the "Boss" (you).
- THE TRACKER (VISION TRACKER): Uses MediaPipe in real-time. It doesn't just see a face; it tracks 478 landmarks to extract:
    - Head Pose: Yaw, Pitch, Roll (Avatar follows your head movement).
    - Eye Gaze: Where you look on the screen.
    - Blink: Detects and mirrors your blinks.
    - Emotions: Extracts smile and concern blendshapes to mirror your mood.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE "HANDS": PROCEDURAL GESTURE ENGINE
Located in app.js, the gesture engine makes JARVIS feel alive. It isn't just playing static animations:

- 8 DYNAMIC STATES: IDLE_BREATHE, TALK_GESTURE, THINKING, POINT_EXPLAIN, OPEN_HANDS, CROSSED_ARMS, SITTING, WALK.
- MICRO-RANDOMIZATION: Every gesture has small random variations (Math.random()) so he never repeats himself exactly.
- LIP-SYNC: Analyzes audio frequencies (FFT) to drive the 3D model's mouth shapes (A, I, U, E, O).
- AMPLITUDE-DRIVEN HANDS: When JARVIS speaks louder/more excitedly, his hand gestures become more emphatic automatically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. REMOTE AUTHORIZATION FLOW
This is the security protocol that allows JARVIS to remain protected:
1. Intruder Detected: Local face scan fails.
2. Telegram Alert: JARVIS snaps a photo and sends it to your Telegram with Approve/Deny buttons.
3. Shadowing: If a guest is approved, JARVIS mirrors the entire conversation to your phone so you can monitor what they are asking.
